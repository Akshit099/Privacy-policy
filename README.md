# Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: September 2025**

We value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled within this application.
## 1. Data Collection
This app does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.
All passwords, notes, and related content are stored locally on your device.
## 2. Data Security
Sensitive data (including saved passwords and secure notes) is encrypted using industry-standard encryption (AES-256).
EncryptedSharedPreferences and Android Keystore are used to protect your information.
Exported files are encrypted with a password you provide and cannot be accessed without it.
## 3. Access Control
Biometric authentication (fingerprint/face unlock) and/or device-level authentication is used to protect access.
Tamper detection features warn you if the application integrity is compromised.
## 4. Import & Export
Exported data is encrypted before leaving the app.
To restore (import) data, you must enter the same password used during export.
The app never sends this data to external servers; import/export is strictly user-controlled.
## 5. Third-Party Services
This app does not include ads or tracking SDKs.
No analytics or external monitoring tools are integrated.
## 6. User Responsibilities
You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your export password.
You should also keep your device secure with system-level protections (PIN, password, biometrics).
## 7. Updates to this Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
Changes will be reflected in this section, with the “Last Updated” date revised accordingly.
