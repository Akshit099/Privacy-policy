# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 2026

## 1. Data Security

Your Google Gemini API Key is stored **locally on your device** using encrypted internal storage (AsyncStorage). We do not transmit or store your API key on any external servers owned by **Prompt Flow**.

## 2. Prompt Data Processing

All prompts and feedback you enter are sent **directly to Google AI Studio** for processing. Prompt Flow does **not** log, save, or monitor your conversation history on any remote database.

## 3. Local Storage

Your iteration history and pinned prompts are saved **only on your device**. If you uninstall the app or clear your storage, this data will be permanently deleted.

## 4. Google’s Policy

By using this app, you are subject to **Google’s Generative AI Terms of Service**. We recommend reviewing how Google handles data sent via their API.

👉 [View Google Generative AI Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai)

## Questions?

If you have concerns about your data, please contact the developer via the **Google Play Store**.
