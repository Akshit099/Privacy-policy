# Prompt Flow Studio (Web) – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 2026

---

## 1. Information We Collect

- **User Inputs & Prompts:** The prompts you submit and any feedback you provide.  
- **Prompt History:** Your recent prompt history may be temporarily processed to enhance AI-generated suggestions.  
- **Local Storage Data:** API keys or preferences you store in your browser for app functionality.  
- **Usage Data:** Technical information such as browser type, device, and usage analytics may be collected anonymously to improve the app.  

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- To generate enhanced prompts and suggestions using AI models.  
- To summarize and manage your prompt history for iterative improvements.  
- To provide personalized functionality, such as restoring recent sessions.  
- To monitor and improve app performance and user experience.  

## 3. Data Handling & Storage

- **Local Storage:** API keys are stored locally on your device for convenience. We do not store your API key on our servers.  
- **Server Processing:** Prompt content and history are processed on our servers only to generate AI responses. We do not share this data with third parties.  
- **Retention:** We do not store full prompt history permanently. Only summaries may be temporarily retained for ongoing sessions.  

## 4. User Control

- You can delete or modify your prompts and feedback at any time in your session.  
- Clearing your browser storage will remove your API key and session data.  

## 5. Security

- All AI requests between your device and our servers are transmitted securely over HTTPS.  
- We do not expose internal AI prompt logic or your API keys to other users.  

## 6. Third-Party Services

- The app uses external AI models for prompt enhancement. While these services process your prompts, your API keys remain on your device and are never shared.  
- No personal data is shared for marketing purposes.  
- By using this app, you are subject to **Google’s Generative AI Terms of Service**. We recommend reviewing how Google handles data sent via their API.
